# Regulatory Research Baseline — 1 September 2026

Primary official sources used for this starter build:
- NIS2: Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (EUR-Lex)
- Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2690
- ENISA NIS2 Technical Implementation Guidance (June 2025)
- GDPR: Regulation (EU) 2016/679, including Article 32
- DORA: Regulation (EU) 2022/2554
- Cyber Resilience Act: Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, including Annex I and Article 14
- EU Cybersecurity Act: Regulation (EU) 2019/881

Rules for production regulatory content:
1. Official EU and Member-State sources are authoritative.
2. Track source URI, article/annex, version, effective date and applicability.
3. Directives require Member-State transposition tracking.
4. DORA Level 2 acts are separate content objects.
5. ENISA guidance assists implementation but does not replace binding law.
6. Every mapping must be versioned and reviewed.
7. No compliance score may hide missing evidence or unresolved applicability.
